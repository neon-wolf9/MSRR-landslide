The `reference_results/` files are **frozen result evidence**, not substitutes for model inputs or proof of an independent rerun. A complete main-result assessment needs either (a) a rights-cleared, source-versioned model-ready package **plus** all exact loader ancillary files/modules and a tested placement guide, or (b) a verifiable independent reconstruction path with the same dependency closure. Synthetic data demonstrates interfaces only.

Do not silently copy private modules or datasets into the public tree. The absent BASE-00 modules and original finalizer are scientific code; their publication needs author provenance/security review and should preserve frozen behavior. Their absence is a real workflow defect, not a data-licence issue.
