## Rights evidence and exact decision still needed

The original Hiroshima prerequisite records `AJG1 == 1 AND GSI == 1`; the finalizer/report records `FINAL_REPAIRED_DATASET_V2_20260818`. The Kyushu frozen README identifies GSI collapse geometry, and the frozen matched table identifies `GSI_D1_NO874_FORMAL_INTERPRETATION`. [GSI's Kyushu event page](https://www.gsi.go.jp/BOUSAI/H29hukuoka_ooita-heavyrain.html) identifies Technical Report D1-No.874 and states that reuse, including republication, is allowed with source credit under GSI content terms. [GSI's terms](https://www.gsi.go.jp/ENGLISH/page_e30286.html) require source/modified-data credit and checking third-party rights; the Japanese text is authoritative. This is positive provider-level evidence, not proof that every frozen grid label or joined table is cleared. The [AJG fourth report](https://www.ajg.or.jp/disaster/files/201807_report004.pdf) states CC BY 4.0, but the exact frozen Hiroshima label edition has not been matched to it. [NASA GPM policy](https://gpm.nasa.gov/data/policy) supports open GPM use with citation, but exact IMERG granule/collection notices remain to be recorded. [OSM copyright](https://www.openstreetmap.org/copyright) requires ODbL analysis of the two mixed static databases. See `DATA_RIGHTS_AUDIT.md` for other providers.

Before copying any of the ten exact files into a public-ready data archive, the author/data steward should sign off: (1) exact source product/version and acquisition record; (2) applicable licence and any exception; (3) attribution/modified-data wording and separate derived-data licence; (4) whether the joined IDs, labels or grid/time series have a disclosure restriction; (5) an exact-file allow/withhold decision. `NEEDS RIGHTS REVIEW` is not a legal prohibition. If not allowed, document the actual restriction clause and lawful acquisition plus exact reconstruction workflow; do not silently substitute synthetic values for paper inputs.
