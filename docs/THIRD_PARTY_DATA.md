The study used the following third-party sources recorded in the project manifests and manuscript materials:

| Source | Product or role | Public-package status |
|---|---|---|
| Association of Japanese Geographers (AJG) | July 2018 Hiroshima slope-failure interpretation | Not redistributed |
| Geospatial Information Authority of Japan (GSI) | Event evidence and elevation products | Not redistributed |
| Copernicus Data Space | Copernicus DEM GLO-30 (`COP-DEM_GLO-30-DGED`) | Not redistributed |
| ISRIC | SoilGrids 2.0 covariates | Not redistributed |
| U.S. Geological Survey | Landsat 8 Collection 2 Level-2 surface reflectance and QA | Not redistributed |

| OpenStreetMap contributors / Geofabrik | Historical roads, waterways, coastline, and water features | Not redistributed; ODbL applies |
| University of Tokyo | J-FlwDir v1.4 hydrological layers | Not redistributed |
| GSJ/AIST | Seamless Digital Geological Map of Japan V2, 1:200,000 | Not redistributed |
| NASA GES DISC | GPM IMERG Final Run V07B (`GPM_3IMERGHH_07`) | Not redistributed; authentication normally required |

Exact versions, event cutoffs, official pages already recorded by the project, expected paths, and acquisition cautions are in `docs/DATA_SOURCES.md`. Provider terms can change; users must verify current licensing and attribution at acquisition time. A current product or snapshot is not automatically equivalent to the frozen study asset.
